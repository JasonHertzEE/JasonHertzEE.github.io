System Overview

View Modules

Modules

I developed all the modules here by studing datasheets, running simulations, and testing configurations.
The system drives a laser diode with approximately 100 mA. This current is controlled with Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) at about 2 kHz. 
It is powered by a transformer plug or rechargeable battery.
A Liquid Crystal Display (LCD) shows the adjustable PWM ratio and current through the laser diode.

Laser Pointer System Diagram

Power Supply

The power supply provides 9 V for the laser diode and 3.3 V for the display, logic, and current regulation. It consists of an ~12V transformer, LDO voltage regulators, a LiPo battery, and a battery protection circuit.
